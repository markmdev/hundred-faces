A hundred faces

Show it to a hundred people before you post it.

Type a post, a DM, an announcement. A hundred invented people react while you type: each is a written profile, and TypeSafe AI's Jev estimates how that person would take it. Four hundred answers per update, back in about half a second.

    How it works

    Every face is a person written in about fifty words: age, job, where they live, temperament, what they care about, how they read. When you pause, the message and the profiles go to Jev, a model from TypeSafe AI that answers typed questions with calibrated probabilities instead of text. Four questions per person: which of seven reactions they'd have, and whether they'd understand it, trust it, and pass it on. Ten calls run in parallel and come back in about half a second. The face is drawn from the whole distribution, so someone who is 60% confused and 40% annoyed looks like that. The counts above the wall are people whose probability is above 50%. Your text goes to Jev and comes back as numbers; it lives in the link and nowhere else.
